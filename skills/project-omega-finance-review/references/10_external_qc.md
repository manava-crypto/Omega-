# External QC (XQC)

External QC is the stage immediately after the Final Review Gate (FRG) and the last check before the task goes to TFRL. Nothing downstream catches an error made here, so every verdict goes through the multi-pass review in §4 before it is sent. Sources: "SOP: External QC (XQC) Workflow for Trainers — RL World" (30 Oct 2026, draft for review) and the External QC rounds in review sessions from 20 to 30 Oct 2026 (task key in §6).

## 1. Workflow

The platform's own guide is `12_external_qc_platform_guide.md`; the verifier fixes are built with the `verifier-json-fix` skill (`skills/verifier-json-fix/`, expert mode). These supersede the 30 Oct SOP's tracker flow, in which the QC team revised the verifier file.

- **Claim.** A task whose Final Review you did stays with you; otherwise claim it from Available for Review. It sits under My Tasks and the claim does not expire during External QC. Before approving at Final Review, make sure the task is final: anything already known to be wrong is fixed then, not left for rework.
- **Awaiting External QC Feedback.** The QC team reviews, fixes small problems itself and sends what needs your expertise. Nothing to do yet. If QC fixes something, the task moves to a new version and you work on that one.
- **Rework Pending.** Open the task from My Tasks, go to the Content tab and click Download artifacts. Answer every item: Agree if the issue is real and needs fixing, Disagree with a note saying why if it is not. Items marked Pass need no answer. Answers save automatically, and answers on a version do not carry over if QC uploads a newer one while you work.
- **Fix and upload.** If you agreed with anything, fix the task in the downloaded copy and click Upload Updated Task: one upload per round, so every fix goes in it. Verifier findings are fixed in `tests/verifier.json` only, with the `verifier-json-fix` skill: it needs the fresh download (its file name and `.external-qc-manifest.json` unchanged, the judges in the download box exactly as the expert guide lists them), QC's `-external-qc-feedback.json` and the runs zip, and it ends `ROUND 1: READY` with the upload file `round1/trainer/<download name>_verifierfix_upload.zip`, a line per QC item and a next-round list. A finding whose cure is in the prompt, golden or data is fixed by the expert in the same copy, the way they usually would.
- **Upload checks.** The platform refuses an upload that adds, removes or renames a file; touches the Dockerfile, `environment/skills/`, `solution/solve.sh`, `tests/test.sh`, `tests/verifier_runtime.py` or `tests/verifier_lib/`; empties a file or leaves a document that will not open; or moves a task that had 15 to 100 verifiers outside that range. An AI check also refuses a change that removes content, changes what the task asks for, makes grading easier, contradicts the task's data or carries notes aimed at a reviewer. The skill adds its own rules: only feedback-named verifiers change in this upload, `task.toml` and the judge configuration stay as downloaded, and weights sum to exactly 1.
- **Submit Verdict.** Disagreeing with everything approves the task. Agreeing with anything sends the fixed task back to QC as a new version and uses one of the task's limited rework rounds; with none left the task is approved or rejected, and a task with too many confirmed issues in one area may be rejected instead of sent back. Automatic quality checks may review your answers, notes and upload first and lock the task while they run.

## 2. What a finding looks like
## 2. What a finding looks like

The screen opens "Rework pending — review QC feedback. Review each item, then submit your verdict. If any items are true positives, upload a corrected package before submitting." The verifier file under review is attached as "verifier (NN).json". Each finding card shows:

- tags: a category ("Several valid readings", "Conflicting requirement", "Unasked requirement", "Wrong golden answer", "Weak grader", "Grader bug"; some cards carry none) plus "Blocking issue" and "Needs Review";
- the header "Review <id> · finding <n> · <x>% of the grade at risk · <criterion or k criteria>";
- the reviewer: CLAUDE-CODE, which cites named solver runs and oracle runs, or QC-MANAGER-READ, which reads the files only and tags its remediation "(Apply only after the domain expert agrees.)";
- Criteria at risk with weights, bullets for Prompt, Criterion, Source and runs, "Why a correct answer fails", "Remediation" (often a verifier fix or a prompt fix), the target file (verifier.json, sometimes also task.toml, instruction.md or a golden file) and Agree / Disagree buttons.

The same defect usually arrives in several cards from different reviews. Some cards bundle several criteria. QC's own warnings ("The quoted words were not found in that file; check them first.") are claims to check, not findings.

## 3. Deciding true positive or false positive

**True positive (Agree).** The finding names a defect that is present in the current verifier.json and needs a change: the answer it describes under "Why a correct answer fails" really is correct under the prompt and the sources, and it really fails the criterion as written (or a wrong answer really passes) for the reason the finding gives.

**False positive (Disagree).** The finding's own complaint does not hold: the answer it calls correct leaves out or contradicts something the prompt asks for; the criterion does not say what the finding quotes; the sources or golden settle the point the finding calls open; or the failure is a grader or infrastructure fault that no verifier.json change cures (judge authentication errors, ties failing pessimistically, a judge whose reasoning says met but scores 0, timeouts, extraction size limits, a document-import deadlock, a sandbox clock later than the scenario date). A grader fault is a Disagree with "No change needed to verifier.json; the fix belongs in the grading infrastructure" and an escalation line (template G). No change needed means false positive.

Rules that decide the close calls:

1. Test the finding's own complaint and its own example of a correct answer, item by item. Never agree with everything by default, and never agree because a different finding exposed a narrower defect.
2. The category tag does not decide the verdict or the layer. A "Wrong golden answer" card whose golden is right and whose criterion is wrong is a verifier finding: Agree, and say the golden is unchanged. A card whose golden is actually wrong is a golden finding: Agree and name the golden change the expert must make.
3. Wrong side claims (a wrong section number, "every answer fails" when the golden passes) do not make a finding false if its complaint holds; correct them inside the Agree note. A valid side point inside a finding whose complaint fails makes it an Agree only if that side point would itself mis-grade a realistic answer; otherwise Disagree and mention the side point in one sentence.
4. A bundled card gets one verdict. Agree when the complaint holds for at least one listed criterion, and name each listed criterion that is a false positive and stays unchanged.
5. Duplicate cards (same criterion, same evidence) get the same verdict and the same fix.
6. Check every factual claim QC makes against the files before agreeing. Solver-run evidence you cannot open is labelled as QC's account, and checked against the current version (judge panel, verifier version, date).
7. Before agreeing that a defect lives in the prompt, sources or golden, look for a sound verifier-only cure: accept every defensible reading the prompt and sources allow, drop a requirement the prompt never asks for, grade presence anywhere instead of a fixed placement, bound a band by the sources. Take that cure when it exists. When the real defect is in the prompt, golden or data, Agree, name the file and the exact change the expert must make, and check that change against the upload's same-task rule: a fix that changes what the task asks for, removes content or makes grading easier is refused, so say so in the note when no allowed fix exists.
8. Decide each remediation item TAKE or REJECT, and name the items that must not be adopted. REJECT a remediation that edits the prompt, sources or golden to cure a defect the verifier file can cure; names or labels the planted item; adds a condition the prompt never sets; is an escape clause; removes a content floor; scores unseen or truncated evidence as a pass; or asks a text-reading judge to do what it cannot ("check the arithmetic against the stated inputs", "later than the current date").
9. Never weaken a sound verifier. Loosen a criterion by adding accepted alternatives, each anchored to a recomputed figure and its convention, and keep its content checks.
10. Reversing your own FRG approval is fine when the runs show it: write "Changed from the Final Review APPROVE because <evidence>" and say plainly that the approval was a miss.
11. If you find a blocking verifier defect QC did not raise, tell the user in one line outside the entries and put it on the next-round list; the upload may change only feedback-named verifiers.

## 4. Multi-pass review (mandatory)

Run every pass on every finding. The `/omega-external-qc` command runs these passes as a multi-agent workflow (`.claude/workflows/omega-external-qc.js`): a shared evidence ledger with the run snapshot, two independent verdict sets, judges on every item where they differ or are unsure, a second judge on any overturn, change or fix outside verifier.json, and a "you sure you agree with all findings?" pass before anything is written. The verdicts handed to the user are final; the user should never need to ask twice. Keep the working notes in the scratchpad; only the result is output.

- **Pass 0, identity and diff.** Match the screen: task ID, the version in the task link, the attached verifier file, the card count. Diff the verifier file against the FRG-approved version and any earlier corrected file. Report agreed edits that did not land and changes nobody requested (whole-workbook judges, a duplicated judge model, a garbled "PASS only if " prefix, a changed judge panel), and escalate missing agreed edits to the pod lead.
- **Pass 1, independent verdict.** For each card, read the criterion verbatim in the current file, the prompt clauses it rests on, the sources and the golden. Recompute every figure QC cites. Grade the finding's example answer against the criterion text yourself. Record the verdict and the deciding evidence.
- **Pass 2, adversarial re-test.** Try to break each verdict. For an Agree: show the example answer is not correct under the prompt, or that the criterion does not say what QC claims. For a Disagree: build a realistic correct answer that fails the current text. A verdict stands only if the attack fails; otherwise flip it and record why.
- **Pass 3, set consistency.** Duplicates and bundles aligned; every verdict routed to its layer; the outcome decided (approved, or a fix and upload); the fix plan covers every agreed finding, and the verifier upload changes only feedback-named verifiers; no two fixes conflict; the golden passes every changed criterion; a correct-value probe passes, a known-wrong probe fails and your own wording has no loophole; any fix that makes a check stricter is flagged.
- **Pass 4, pre-send.** One entry per card in screen order, each headed exactly as the screen shows it, with the card text pasted verbatim into any delegation brief; every button word matches its note (Agree means a defect is present, with proof and a fix in the request; Disagree means the item is clean, with the reason, the proof and "No change needed"); tallies come from the entries; no FRG field verdicts and no "Overall REJECT" line.

When the review is delegated, run two reviewers independently, neither seeing the other's verdicts, and settle every split by re-deriving it from the files, never by vote. When several agents draft fixes, fix one shared policy (bands, caps, anchors) before drafting and reconcile centrally. Expect "are you sure you agree with all findings?": answer it in advance by naming the closest call in the lead. When challenged, re-test against the same bar and change a verdict only with "changed because <new evidence>" or "changed because the bar was misapplied"; one new fact never flips unrelated findings.

## 5. Output

- **Lead**, one short paragraph: the outcome (every item disagreed, so the task is approved; or a fix and upload this round), the tally, and the closest call.
- **One entry per item**, in screen order, headed exactly as on screen: "Review 355969 · finding 3 — DISAGREE." An Agree is one or two sentences: the defect present now with its quoted locator, any corrected side claim, the file the fix goes in, and "Changed from the Final Review APPROVE because …" where that applies. A Disagree is one paragraph, two at most: the reason, the proof (criterion quote, prompt clause, source cell or figure) and "No change needed."; a grader or infrastructure fault adds an escalation line.
- **Fix plan** when anything is agreed: for verifier items, the brief for the `verifier-json-fix` skill (the download, the feedback file, the runs zip, and per item the agreed change); for prompt, golden or data items, the exact change the expert makes, each checked against the upload rules. After the skill's round, relay its upload file name, its one line per QC item (the text to paste where RL World asks what changed) and its next-round list.
- **Escalations**, one template G line each. No Final Review field verdicts and no Overall line.
- An overall note, when asked, is one short paragraph with no bullets.

## 6. Lessons from the first XQC rounds

Task key: LHF (lh-finance_and_insurance-financial_and_investment_analysts-20260914-103056) and the as-* tasks by suffix: p8f6vjxz-0, ohv1n4ti-2, jicq4o2h-1, 9jv02sfa-3, lxwjwnav-1, p98yff3p-8, 3m6bdh9w-3, dr4hyy6f-0, dr4hyy6f-2, dr4hyy6f-4.

- **Final Review approvals came back as true positives.** External QC reviews (solver runs or file reads) showed correct answers failing on requirements the prompt never asks for: a literal "$6,640,000" and "total GP fee" (LHF, 14–21% of the grade), "405.96255m" and a £123.616m subtotal (dr4hyy6f-4), a closed owner list "General Counsel, the Treasurer, or the CFO" when the packet has no Treasurer and the golden's own "Treasury / CAO" fails it (dr4hyy6f-0), a single accepted reading when the prompt invites the other (reopening LW-Q3H8, p8f6vjxz-0; EBITDA 529.5992 for 4.578934x, ohv1n4ti-2), a provider's 4.2–4.5 year band the prompt asks the solver to challenge (p98yff3p-8), golden wording that fails its own FAIL clause (A95 "backlog (earliest demand) > current-month demand", dr4hyy6f-2; A06/A07 labels, 3m6bdh9w-3) and a pass case that breaches the governing standard (IAS 33.36/.38 dilution, dr4hyy6f-4). At FRG these are blocking mis-grades, not optional placement readings.
- **Agree-all by default was reversed under challenge.** LHF finding 4 (the prompt asks how sensitive the answer "is to unresolved terms"), three market-alignment findings in jicq4o2h-1 (the cited answer would still fail correctly after any fix) and three in ohv1n4ti-2.
- **Fixes went to the prompt.** LHF added a sentence to instruction.md and lxwjwnav-1 added a scenario date. Both had verifier-only cures that were not taken; a prompt change must also pass the upload's same-task check.
- **Grader faults were agreed with no change** (9jv02sfa-3, six findings). No verifier change means false positive: Disagree and escalate.
- **The wrong deliverable.** jicq4o2h-1 handed over a revised verifier.json; the user wanted verdicts and "a single comment" for the QC team.
- **Regeneration lost agreed edits** (3m6bdh9w-3 v43, dr4hyy6f-2 v41, p8f6vjxz-0 v45, ohv1n4ti-2 v47) and added unrequested changes. Diff first, every round.
- **Out-of-scope extras.** A deleted verifier with re-scaled weights and edits outside the findings went into corrected files, and missing Final Review fixes were folded in without being named as such (jicq4o2h-1, 9jv02sfa-3, dr4hyy6f-0).
- **A fixed date the prompt never states.** lxwjwnav-1's Final Review rejected deck_close_action_plan over a 21 October step; External QC then failed it again because the prompt gives no current date and the runs' sandbox clock read 28 October.
- **Subagent drift.** p98yff3p-8's three drafting agents set the term cap at 5.37, 5.4 and 5.25 years; lxwjwnav-1's subagent wrote "finding 3" for the screen's "finding 1".

## 7. Open points

The platform guide lets the expert fix the prompt, golden or data at External QC, while the `verifier-json-fix` skill states that RL World wants verifier issues fixed in `verifier.json` only and refuses uploads that change unnamed verifiers. The working rule here: verifier items through the skill, other items by the expert, every change inside the upload checks. Whether a bundled card is a true positive when only some of its criteria are, and whether an Agree needs a note, are not stated anywhere; the rules in §3 are the practice from the sessions.
